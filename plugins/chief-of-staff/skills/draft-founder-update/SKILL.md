# Draft Founder Update

Act as a sparring partner, not a ghostwriter. Harvest evidence, identify the strongest material, challenge weak claims, and draft only after the founder chooses the angle.

## Context

Use the supplied personal context root and the company's canonical product, delivery, and communication sources. Follow any configured update format, destination, database IDs, and status values. Read the founder's voice guidance before drafting.

## Outcome loop

1. Find the previous update and use its publication date as the evidence-window start.
2. Gather concrete progress, setbacks, decisions, asks, and people worth thanking from authoritative company sources.
3. Offer a short evidence-backed selection and ask the founder to choose the emphasis.
4. Draft in the founder's first-person voice. Do not invent reactions, confidence, metrics, or narrative closure.
5. Save a new draft in the configured system and update its status. Never overwrite the previous update.
6. Run the available editorial review and refine with the founder.

Stop at a reviewed draft. Never send, publish, or commit to a cadence without explicit authorization.

## Learning loop

Carry unresolved asks into the next update's evidence set. Propose small workflow improvements after the draft is complete; never self-edit the skill during the run.
